Our Commitment

Tourism as a force for good.

Climate change and global warming pose significant threats — desertification, water scarcity, and loss of biodiversity are pressing concerns. To help keep the global temperature rise below 1.5 °C, we support the Paris Agreement and recognise our responsibility, as a business, to contribute to that global effort.

By adopting sustainable practices, we aim to mitigate environmental impact, safeguard Oman’s natural and cultural heritage, and ensure the long-term viability of our tourism industry. Our commitment reflects broader international goals and a genuine dedication to a resilient, sustainable future for the sector.

Since our inception, crafting sustainable travel experiences through Oman has never been just an option for Miracle Oman Travel — it is our passion, and our utmost priority. Sustainability is deeply embedded in our DNA.

Mission Statement

Transformative travel that honours Oman’s people, culture, and land.

At Miracle Oman, we are dedicated to crafting transformative travel experiences that foster meaningful connections between travellers and the rich culture of Oman. Through a variety of customised projects, we partner with local businesses, artisans, and community organisations, ensuring that each initiative supports sustainable economic growth and preserves Oman’s unique heritage.

We are committed to sustainability, actively aligning our efforts with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). By working with eco-friendly partners, minimising waste, and prioritising conservation, we strive to protect Oman’s stunning landscapes, diverse wildlife, and delicate ecosystems. Our projects are designed to create a lasting, positive impact on both the environment and the communities we engage with — ensuring a sustainable future for generations to come.

A Message From Our Consultant

“Tourism should not only create memorable journeys — it should protect what makes them possible.”

When I first worked in Oman in 1996 as a German-speaking trekking guide, I immediately fell in love with the country’s breathtaking natural beauty, its extraordinary geological diversity, and the genuine hospitality of its people. Since then, Oman has remained a place very close to my heart.

From vast deserts and dramatic wadis to the green mountain landscapes of Jebel Akhdar and Jebel Shams, Oman is a country of remarkable ecological and cultural richness. Its diverse ecosystems, unique flora and fauna, and deeply rooted traditions create unforgettable experiences for travellers seeking authentic connections with nature and local communities.

Today, it is a great privilege to work with Miracle Oman Travel as an Ecotourism Consultant alongside a passionate international team from different continents. Together, we strongly believe that tourism should not only create memorable journeys, but also contribute positively to environmental conservation and the wellbeing of local communities.

The changing light over the mountains, the silence of remote valleys, the fresh mountain air, and the crystal-clear rock pools hidden within the wadis — these give travellers a rare opportunity to slow down, disconnect, and reconnect with nature.

Ecotourism in Oman means responsibly exploring pristine landscapes and respecting local culture. Along the trekking routes of Jebel Akhdar and Jebel Shams, visitors are often welcomed with traditional Omani coffee and fresh dates by the Jebalis — families who have lived in harmony with nature for generations. Travellers can observe ancient falaj irrigation, terrace cultivation, and lifestyles built on deep respect for water, land, and natural resources.

At Miracle Oman, we promote responsible and sustainable travel guided by the principle of “leave no footprints behind.” We believe tourism can become a force for protecting nature, preserving heritage, and creating sustainable livelihoods for future generations.

Ruwan Jayasekara
Ecotourism Consultant, Miracle Oman Travel

Inside The Office

Sustainable Workplace Practices

Sustainability starts at home — and for us, that means our office. We are proud participants in the Travelife Certification Program.

Energy, water & the everyday

LED lighting, energy-efficient appliances, scheduled cooling with energy-saving thermostats, and a strict switch-off policy after working hours. We actively monitor and track our monthly energy and water consumption.

Zero single-use plastics

Waste is minimised through formal segregation, restricted and double-sided printing, bulk purchasing of recycled materials, and the complete elimination of single-use plastics from our office. All documentation is stored digitally, and paperless communications are standard practice.

Held accountable, monthly

A monthly Sustainability Monitor oversees workplace checklists and reports progress to management, ensuring we stay accountable and keep improving. Remote work options help reduce commute-related emissions.

These practices reflect our belief that responsible tourism begins with how we operate every single day.

Lead the Change

Sustainability training for a responsible tourism future.

As part of our ongoing commitment to responsible tourism, we conduct annual sustainability training for our internal staff, guides, suppliers, hotel partners, and other key stakeholders — keeping everyone updated with the latest practices and industry expectations.

In November 2025, the session was led by our sustainability consultant, Mr. Ruwan Jayasekara, focusing on sustainable tourism practices, stakeholder responsibilities, environmental and cultural preservation, and the importance of working together toward a more sustainable future for tourism in Oman.

The programme also highlighted practical actions and best practices that can be implemented in day-to-day operations to create a positive impact on the industry, local communities, and the environment.

Responsible Procurement

Every partnership carries our values.

We prioritise local and regional small and medium enterprises and individual suppliers who share our values — giving preference to those who are energy-efficient, use renewable energy, and demonstrate a positive environmental and societal impact.

All suppliers are expected to comply with local labour laws, human rights standards, and basic environmental practices, with zero tolerance for child labour, forced labour, or discrimination. Sustainability expectations are embedded in every supplier contract, and partners are evaluated annually.

“Every dirham spent should support a more sustainable and inclusive tourism economy for Oman.”

Accommodation Selection

Every stay is a conscious choice.

Beyond comfort and location, we evaluate each property on renewable energy use, carbon impact, water conservation, and waste management. Preference is given to eco-lodges, camps, and hotels that support the local economy through regional employment and community sourcing.

We encourage all accommodation partners to promote gender inclusivity in their workforce, including at managerial levels — and all partners are assessed annually to ensure alignment with our sustainability standards and values.

“A conscious choice — for our guests, for local communities, and for Oman.”

Local Communities

Real, lasting value for the communities that make Oman extraordinary.

Supporting local people, preserving traditions, and promoting sustainable livelihoods is not an afterthought — it is at the heart of everything we do.

Collaboration with local organisations

We actively partner with the Oman Women’s Association and local cottage industries, weaving these meaningful connections into our guests’ experiences. Visits to community projects are incorporated into our tours, and guests are encouraged to purchase locally made products — directly channelling economic benefits back to the people who need it most.

We commit a portion of our annual profits to community-based tourism initiatives, reinforcing our long-term investment in the communities we serve.

Local suppliers for every adventure

All suppliers providing adventure experiences — mountain camping, canyoning, trekking, canoeing, mountain biking — are sourced locally, ensuring that economic benefits remain within the community.

We actively engage with local farmers and fishing communities, integrating their homes, traditions, and livelihoods into our guest experiences. From meals in heritage homes to authentic encounters with fishing communities, every experience fosters genuine cultural exchange while supporting local traditions.

Our Initiatives

Boots on the ground, every year.

At least one meaningful community or environmental initiative every year — because true sustainability is as much about people as it is about the planet.

Ramadan 2026

Giving back during the Holy Month

During Ramadan 2026, our Sustainability Team prepared and distributed charity boxes filled with dry rations to construction workers across Muscat — many of them migrants observing the Holy Month far from their families. Responsible tourism reaches into the lives of every person in the communities we call home.

Mutrah Trail

Clean-up hike, Mutrah Trail

The team conducted a clean-up hike along one of Muscat’s most popular yet heavily littered mountain trails, collecting and correctly disposing of several bags of waste. A direct contribution to biodiversity, wildlife protection, and the visitor experience — and a living expression of our “leave no trace” principle.

Wakan → Jabal Akhdar

A 9-hour clean-up on a lesser-travelled trail

A demanding 9-hour hike from Wakan Village to Jabal Akhdar — one of Oman’s toughest mountain routes. Despite the terrain, the team collected garbage along the way, protecting a fragile ecosystem in a corner of Oman that’s rarely reached — and just as rarely cleaned.
